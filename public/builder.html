How it works

Enter a fund amount. It is split 40% index ETFs, 40% value stocks (Deep value screen) and 20% momentum stocks (top 15 of the Momentum screen), equal weight inside each part, using the same data and screens as Fundamentals. PSU stocks are never picked (value or momentum). The index part holds two ETF/Gold slots (IT/Gold and ALPHA/Gold): a slot holds Gold while its ETF is not stronger, so Gold can be held twice (2× weight). The backtest applies the same rules as they stood 1 year ago, and re-checks the ETF pairs every month.
