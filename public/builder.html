How it works

Enter a fund amount. It is split between index ETFs, value stocks (Deep value screen) and momentum stocks (top 15 of the Momentum screen), equal weight inside each part, using the same data and screens as the Factor Screener. The split is 40 / 40 / 20 by default: type the ETF % and the Value % and Momentum gets the rest. For the Value and Momentum parts you can pick the list (universe), what to sort by (value score, momentum score, PE or PB, either way) and how many stocks to take; the sort always uses the numbers of the date shown (today, or 1 year back in the backtest). PSU stocks are never picked (value or momentum). The index part holds two ETF/Gold slots (IT/Gold and ALPHA/Gold): a slot holds Gold while its ETF is not stronger, so Gold can be held twice (2× weight). The backtest applies the same rules as they stood 1 year ago, and re-checks the ETF pairs every month.
