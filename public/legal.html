About HNImanshu

Last updated: 22 October 2026

HNImanshu is software, not advice. It is a stock-screening and portfolio-tracking tool for learning and research. It does not recommend, advise or tip any stock, and never tells anyone to buy, sell or hold a security. HNImanshu is not registered with SEBI as an investment adviser or research analyst, and offers no advisory, portfolio-management or tip service of any kind.

What the product is

HNImanshu is a web application (software as a service) that takes the published financial results and market prices of companies listed on the National Stock Exchange of India and lets users sort, filter and study them with their own settings. Paid plans give access to more software features; they do not buy opinions, calls, targets or tips.

  • Valuation and Factor Screener: tables of companies with ratios worked out from public data (for example price-to-earnings, debt, growth), which the user filters and sorts with their own limits.
  • Portfolio (paper / tracking): users type in their own transactions to track their own holdings and profit or loss. Nothing is bought or sold through HNImanshu, and we do not hold or handle any user's money or securities.
  • Checklist: shows how many of a fixed, published set of numeric checks a company's reported figures pass. It is a counting tool, not a rating or a recommendation.
  • Portfolio Builder (backtest): lets the user apply rules they choose to past data and see how such rules would have performed historically. Past results do not predict future returns.
  • News on Telegram: news headlines about the stocks in the user's own portfolio, found through Google's news search and sent on Telegram. Headlines only: no tips, calls or opinions of ours.

Data comes from public sources and can be late, incomplete or wrong. Users make their own decisions and should consult a SEBI-registered adviser before investing.

Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal data HNImanshu collects, why, where it is kept and what you can ask us to do with it. It follows India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000. The data fiduciary is Himanshu Shekhar (contact details below).

1. What we collect

  • Account: the username you choose, or, if you sign in with Google, your name and email address as Google shares them. We also keep when the account was made and when you last signed in. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store them.
  • Paper portfolio: the entries you type in (stock, quantity, price, date, paper pay-ins and pay-outs, the charge rates you set). These are practice figures; no money or securities move through HNImanshu.

  • Visitor counts: a count of people online and a one-way code (hash) of your IP address so each visitor is counted once. The IP address itself is not stored.
  • On your device: the browser keeps your settings (day / night look, chosen tabs and filters), a note that you are signed in, and a copy of PF Builder results so pages open faster. This copy is deleted when you log out.
